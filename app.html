Caddy Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts an event's carnival cupcakes with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the caddies the cupcakes take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a cupcake, and the minutes the packing takes. It uses no named constants. The cupcakes, the caddy size, the weekly use, the caddy price and the packing speed are yours to decide and read off your own orders, shelves and invoices. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your cupcakes, your caddies or your venue and does not know whether the cupcakes will really stay fresh, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a caddy is worth its price or a cupcake is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

The caddies truth

Caddies: the cupcakes divided by the cupcakes a caddy, rounded up, with what the last caddy holds.

The weeks truth

Weeks: the cupcakes divided by the cupcakes used a week, with the cupcakes left for the next week.

The cost truth

Cost: the caddies times the caddy price, to the cent, and spread over the cupcakes.

The time truth

Time: the cupcakes divided by the cupcakes packed a minute, rounded up, in minutes and in hours and minutes.
