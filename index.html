Caddy Math

The 290 cupcakes at 12 cupcakes a caddy take 25 caddies, the last caddy holding 2 cupcakes.

Boundary: arithmetic only Caddy Math counts an event's carnival cupcakes with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the caddies the cupcakes take, the weeks the stock lasts, the cost a cupcake, and the minutes the packing takes. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your cupcakes, your caddies or your venue and does not know whether the cupcakes will really stay fresh, the supplier will run out or the price will hold, and it gives no verdict that a caddy is worth its price or a cupcake is right for your event. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 290 cupcakes, 12 cupcakes a caddy, 22 cupcakes a week, $13.50 a caddy and 5 cupcakes a minute.
